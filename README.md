### Research

**TRACE: Graph-Aware Anomaly Detection for Trust Routing in Adversarial Decentralized Marketplaces**
S. Gupta, N. Kaushik, S. Vashisht, R. Bhatt · *presented at ACM CIKM 2026, Rome* · [code](https://github.com/Siddhanthguptaa/TRACE-API)

EigenTrust sends **81.7%** of jobs to Sybil identities under coordinated attack. TRACE cuts collusion-ring fraud by **86%** and is **14× lower** than EigenTrust at 5,000 agents.
